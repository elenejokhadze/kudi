Privacy policy

Last updated 17 Oct 2026

1. Who we are

Kudi is a pet-care app for families. This policy explains what information Kudi collects when you use the app, how it is used and the choices you have.

2. Information you give us

When you set up Kudi you enter details about your pet (name, species, breed, sex, birthday and weight) and your care routine: meal times, medicine, vaccines, heat cycles and trips. When family members join, Kudi records who logged each meal, dose or walk and when. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a Google account ID, and use them only to sign you in. You can also add a photo of your pet; it stays on your phone.

3. Where your information is stored

Right now your records are stored only in this browser on this device. They are not sent to Kudi or anyone else. If you turn on family sharing, your records are stored on Kudi's servers so your family can see them.

4. How we use it

We use your information only to run the app: to show your pet's day, send the reminders you switch on, keep your family in sync and show your progress. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Reminders and calendars

Reminders are shown by your phone while Kudi is running. When you add a reminder to your calendar, Kudi opens your calendar app with the details filled in; nothing is saved there until you tap Save.

6. Pawmates

If you use Pawmates, other members nearby can see your pet's name, age, breed and the short description you write. Messages you send are visible to the person you send them to.

7. Partner links

Some screens link to partner sites such as Amazon, Chewy and pet insurers. If you buy something, Kudi may earn a small fee. We do not send your pet's records to partners. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your records until you delete them. Deleting your account removes every record for you and your family.

9. Your choices

You can change or delete any record at any time. To delete everything, go to Settings and choose Delete my account. You can switch reminders off in Settings, then Reminders.

10. Children

Kudi is meant for adults caring for pets. Children can help log care inside a family, but accounts are created by an adult.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date below and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

12. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Write to us at privacy@kudi.app.
