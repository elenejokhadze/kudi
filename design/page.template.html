Kudi

Healthy care for happy tails. Screens stay white and calm. Deep forest green, lime and cream carry the brand, and a warm blush pink picks out small details.

How much colour

Most of a screen is white, grey and green-black text. Lime marks the action you most want people to take, pink picks out small details, and one deep-green card per screen carries the brand.

Background 62% Grey 20% Ink 10% Lime Green card
Lime: one per screenThe primary button, the active progress bar, today's bar in a chart.
Pink: small detailsBadges, notification dots, hearts, the active tab dot and selected chips. Never a button, a card or a headline.
Forest: text, brand and linksText is a green-black; deep green for links, the selected tab icon and success.
Pastels: small tiles onlyCategory icon tiles and status pills. Never a full card or a page background.
One deep-green feature cardAt most one per screen: cream text, lime for the data, and one pink detail at most.

Buttons

Four buttons, all pill-shaped and 48px tall (56px in the bottom action bar). Lime with dark forest text is the primary button and the most prominent colour in the app.

Sizes and helpers

Colour tokens

Primitives are raw scales. Screens use the semantic tokens, which change for dark mode. The contrast score is measured live.

Semantic
Pink details

Small, warm touches. Each one is a dot, ring, badge or outline, never a fill bigger than a chip.

Pet-care categories

Pale tile, coloured icon. This is where most of the brand colour lives.

Primitives

Icons

One pack: Lucide, rounded outline icons on a 24px grid. Draw them at 20px (16px in chips, 24px in the tab bar) with the default 2px stroke, in currentColor. No hand-drawn icons and no second pack; if Lucide has no fitting icon, pick the closest one.

Typography

Figtree, a clean geometric sans used by many current apps. Weights stay at 400 to 700. Big numbers use medium weight with tight tracking, the way the temperature does in the reference.

Spacing

A 4px scale. 16 is the screen gutter and card padding, 12 the gap between cards, 24 the gap between sections.

One phone-width column centred on wide screens. The top bar holds back and actions; detail screens end with a fixed action bar of primary + secondary button; main screens use the tab bar instead.
