Kudi

Good care, for good pets. Forest-green ink on warm cream, with mint, sunshine, olive, blush and coral taken from the retro dog illustrations.

01 · Colour

Palette

Primitives are raw scales. Screens never use them directly; they use the semantic tokens below, which swap for dark mode.

Semantic tokens
Pet-care categories

Each care type keeps one colour everywhere: its icon tile, chip, calendar dot and reminder.

02 · Typography

Figtree, one family

A rounded geometric sans that is common in current product UI. Headings use heavy weights and tight tracking so they sit close to the chunky wordmark; body copy stays regular.

03 · Spacing

4px base scale

Use these for padding and gaps. 16 is the screen gutter and card padding, 12 the gap between cards, 24 the gap between sections.

04 · Radius

Soft, sticker-like corners

05 · Stroke & elevation

Mostly flat, with a sticker outline

The illustrations are flat colour inside a forest outline. The sticker style (2px outline plus a flat offset) is saved for the primary button and the hero; everything else uses a hairline and a soft shadow.

One phone-width column

The app is a single 480px column centred on wide screens, with a sticky header, a floating bottom tab bar and bottom sheets for every add or edit form.

   mint sky, sunshine yellow, olive, blush pink, coral bones and warm cream.

   Font: Figtree (Google Fonts), weights 400-800. */
